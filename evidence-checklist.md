# Evidence checklist

Keep real evidence in restricted storage. Use references in the workbook rather than publishing exports or credentials.

- [ ] Scope and reviewer responsibilities are recorded before sign-off.
- [ ] Raw population export is preserved with source, timestamp, timezone and filters.
- [ ] Guests, service accounts, privileged roles and disabled accounts are accounted for; omitted categories and limitations are explicit.
- [ ] Unique-account totals reconcile to the workbook; multiple-role rows and duplicates are explained.
- [ ] Current roster, business need and original approvals are referenced where available; missing approvals are investigated rather than invented.
- [ ] Each decision identifies its reviewer and date.
- [ ] Removal/change tickets record the action, operator, completion time and verification.
- [ ] Session, token and indirect/group access effects are checked where relevant to the system.
- [ ] Open exceptions name an accountable owner, risk decision, interim measure and expiry.
- [ ] The reviewer's own privileges have a separate reviewer where feasible; any limitation is documented.
- [ ] Sign-off describes outstanding remediation accurately and references the final workbook version.
- [ ] Evidence access, retention and disposal follow the organization's policy and obligations.

A screenshot alone may omit account populations, filters or timestamps. Preserve exports and system records where available; explain the provenance and limitations of each artifact. Never manufacture screenshots or retroactively represent a review as having occurred earlier.
